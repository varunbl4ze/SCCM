// About the platform

A public ledger for civic issues, not a black box.

Civic Registry gives residents a direct, transparent channel to report problems in shared spaces — roads, water, sanitation, lighting — and gives municipal staff a structured queue instead of scattered phone calls and paperwork.

Filed like a record

Every report gets a permanent docket number you can reference anytime.

Traceable end-to-end

Follow a complaint from submission to resolution with a visible timeline.

Built for shared spaces

Designed around civic and environmental upkeep — the commons we all rely on.

Open to every resident

No department jargon required — plain forms anyone can fill out.
